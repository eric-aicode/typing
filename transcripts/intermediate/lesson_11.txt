The 30th anniversary of Karaage Kun.
Deep fried karaage chicken has been a much-loved staple at convenience stores in Japan for decades.
One of the longest-running varieties is Lawson's karaoke.
Currently celebrating it's 30th anniversary.
To Mark the occasion the convenience store chain is producing a range of special flavors.
Which are accented by Yuzu Pawn Japanese citrus-flavored soy sauce.
