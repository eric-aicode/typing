A good year for smelly fruit.
It May look delicious, but this fruits pungent smell makes it an acquired taste.
Durians the so-called king of fruits are something of a national Obsession in Singapore.
And 2015 is shaping up to be a very good year for durian lovers.
With a bumper crop in neighboring Malaysia.
That means prices are coming down and sales are going up.
Much to the Delight of buyers and sellers alike.
