Tomatoes are good for your skin.
Tomatoes contain a high level of lycopene.
Which is a substance that is used in pricey facial cleansers.
If you want to try using real tomatoes for skin.
You need to start with about 8 to 12 of them.
Remove the skins from the tomatoes.
Then, place them on your face with the inside of the peels touching your skin.
Leave the Tomato peel on your face for a minimum of 10 minutes then rinse.
Your face will feel smooth and clean.
