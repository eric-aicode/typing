The Fox and the grapes part 2.
He jumped and jumped to reach them, but always came up short.
They were too high and just Out Of Reach he stretched and hopped, but to no avail.
No, matter how hard he tried the fox could not reach the grapes.
Giving up finally he said as he walked away.
Those Grapes surely must be sour.
I wouldn't eat them even if they were served to me on a golden platter.
It's easy to despise what you cannot have.
