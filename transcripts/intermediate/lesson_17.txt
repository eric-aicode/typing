The Boy Who Cried Wolf Part 2.
Being a trickster Peter decided to pull the same trick.
A week later with the same result.
Unfortunately, when a real wolf showed up a further week later.
The villagers didn't believe Peter when he once again shouted wolf wolf.
And so Peter was helpless to stop the wolf.
From devastating the entire flock.
The lesson of the story one shouldn't lie.
Blessed others not believe you when you finally tell the truth.
