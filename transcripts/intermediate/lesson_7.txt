The Fox and the grapes part 1.
Long long ago a fox lived close to a Vineyard.
And used to stare at the lovely grapes that hung.
How juicy they look they look as though they'd melt in your mouth if only I could reach them.
1 sunny day the fox woke up and saw the grapes glistening in the sunlight.
The Grapes looked so luscious.
That the famished Fox could no longer control himself.
