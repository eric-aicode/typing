Robbery in Paris.
A gang of Thieves crashed it's 4 by 4 into the front door of a Chanel Boutique in central Paris.
The three thieves took purses worth thousands of pounds.
Then, set the car on fire and drove away on scooters.
Police now have possession of the car and are going to analyze it.
This is not the first robbery of it's kind.
A similar break-in also took place at a Chanel Boutique in London last January.
