Sisters meet after 30 years.
Two sisters were ages 3 and 9 when a volcano in Bolivia erupted.
Leaving more than 20,000 people dead.
After the volcano destroyed their Hometown the two sisters were put up for adoption.
30 years later one of the sisters openly inquired about the whereabouts of her birth family in a Facebook video.
Which was seen by the other sister.
Now reunited the two have a lot of catching up to do.
